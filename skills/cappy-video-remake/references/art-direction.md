# Soft illustrated art direction

For pose performance, contact, movement curves and shot staging adapted from the user's Thanh Hai 2D Studio plugin, read [animation-craft.md](animation-craft.md). Apply its selective transfer within the art direction below.

The user wants further improvements to soft rounded artwork. This preference is established; any new design remains a revision candidate until reviewed, not an already approved style.

Aim for premium illustrated 2.5D objects through purposeful drawing, not more decoration:

- Give each semantic subject a recognizable silhouette and refined organic curves. Avoid representing every different object as a flat circle or a pile of rings.
- Distinguish materials through shape, edge treatment and restrained surface detail: the viewer should recognize what the narration refers to before reading its short label.
- Keep a coherent key-light direction and restrained rim light. Control highlight size and brightness, with shadows and occlusion establishing depth rather than indiscriminate glow.
- Establish value hierarchy: foreground subject first, supporting objects second, moving background third. Separate overlapping forms without excessive contrast, shiny edges or clutter.
- Use highlights, thickness and layered depth where they clarify form. Background textures and extra particles do not compensate for weak subject drawing.
- In scientific cutaways, audit occlusion as part of accuracy. For example, added disk hot spots must not shine through a black hole's depicted dark center: mask the emitting layers or redraw the opaque center above them. Inspect a rendered motion segment, since a single still may miss a moving overlap.
- Preserve the approved white doodle Cappy identity and readable expressions. Improve integration with the scene without redesigning the mascot.

Compare actual rendered before/after frames at matching moments, both full-size and small-size. Check silhouette recognition, materials, lighting consistency, label readability and whether the main idea is immediately clear. Also retain the motion-clip review: richer shading must not hide causal action or destabilize moving labels. Improve existing drawing/rendering tools first; do not add costly libraries merely to suggest higher quality.

Keep all established timing, visible layered-background motion, four-track music trimming/mixing, labels and section-heading requirements. Report the current revision and what was actually verified; do not claim user approval for a new design or promise unattended future improvement. Persist specific user-approved lessons after review rather than turning untested art choices into permanent rules.
